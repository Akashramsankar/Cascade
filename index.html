How to play

Spell words

Drag (or tap) through touching tiles to spell a word of 3+ letters. Diagonals count. Each word is one move.

CAT

Oil & water

After every word, the columns it touched close up. Vowels rise to the top and consonants sink to the bottom. New tiles drop in from the chutes above.

Drop the weights

Three weights sit on the floor. A weight breaks loose once its column holds more vowels than consonants. The meter under each one keeps count.

Cascades

If shifting tiles line up an everyday word by themselves (reading → or ↓), it clears automatically. Cascades are free and can chain.
